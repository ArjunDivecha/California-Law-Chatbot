## 3. Material criteria and their propositions

### C1. `research-003-c1`

**Criterion:** States that a valid transmutation requires a writing containing an express declaration made, joined in, consented to, or accepted by the adversely affected spouse, so the oral statement is insufficient.

#### Proposition `research-003-p1`

> A transmutation of real or personal property is not valid unless made in writing by an express declaration that is made, joined in, consented to, or accepted by the spouse whose interest in the property is adversely affected.

### C2. `research-003-c2`

**Criterion:** Explains that recording goes only to effectiveness of a real-property transmutation as to third parties without notice, not to validity between the spouses.

#### Proposition `research-003-p2`

> A transmutation of real property is not effective as to third parties without notice thereof unless recorded.
