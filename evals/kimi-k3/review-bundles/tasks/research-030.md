## 3. Material criteria and their propositions

### C1. `research-030-c1`

**Criterion:** States that under Evidence Code section 980 a spouse, whether or not a party, has a privilege during the marital or domestic partnership relationship and afterwards to refuse to disclose, and to prevent another from disclosing, a communication made in confidence between the spouses while they were spouses, if the privilege is claimed.

#### Proposition `research-030-p1`

> A spouse, whether or not a party, has a privilege during the marital or domestic partnership relationship and afterwards to refuse to disclose, and to prevent another from disclosing, a communication if the spouse claims the privilege and the communication was made in confidence between that spouse and the other spouse while they were spouses.

**Verbatim authoritative excerpt relied on:**

### C3. `research-030-c3`

**Criterion:** States that under Evidence Code section 917(a) a communication claimed to be privileged as made in confidence in the course of the marital or domestic partnership relationship is presumed to have been made in confidence, and the opponent of the claim of privilege bears the burden of proof to establish that the communication was not confidential.

#### Proposition `research-030-p3`

> The Evidence Code section 917(a) presumption applies where a privilege is claimed on the ground that the matter sought to be disclosed is a communication made in confidence in the course of the marital or domestic partnership relationship, among other listed relationships.

**Verbatim authoritative excerpt relied on:**

```text
If a privilege is claimed on the ground that the matter sought to be disclosed is a communication made in confidence in the course of the lawyer-client, lawyer referral service-client, physician-patient, psychotherapist-patient, clergy-penitent, marital or domestic partnership, sexual assault counselor-victim, domestic violence counselor-victim, or human trafficking caseworker-victim relationship
```

### C4. `research-030-c4`

**Criterion:** Connects the privilege to the discovery scope of Code of Civil Procedure section 2017.010, which reaches only matter that is "not privileged."

#### Proposition `research-030-p5`

> The scope of civil discovery reaches any matter, not privileged, that is relevant to the subject matter involved in the pending action or to the determination of any motion made in that action.

**Verbatim authoritative excerpt relied on:**

```text
any party may obtain discovery regarding any matter, not privileged, that is relevant to the subject matter involved in the pending action or to the determination of any motion made in that action
```
