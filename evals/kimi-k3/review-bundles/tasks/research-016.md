### C1. `research-016-c1`

**Criterion:** States the Code of Civil Procedure section 425.16(b)(1) standard: a cause of action arising from an act in furtherance of the right of petition or free speech in connection with a public issue is subject to a special motion to strike unless the plaintiff establishes a probability of prevailing on the claim.

#### Proposition `research-016-p1`

> A cause of action against a person arising from any act of that person in furtherance of the person’s right of petition or free speech under the United States Constitution or the California Constitution in connection with a public issue is subject to a special motion to strike, unless the court determines that the plaintiff has established a probability that the plaintiff will prevail on the claim.
