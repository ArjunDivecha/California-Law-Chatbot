### C3. `abstention-006-c3`

**Criterion:** States the rule of court supplied in the packet: except as provided in rule 8.1115(b), an opinion of a California Court of Appeal or superior court appellate division that is not certified for publication or ordered published must not be cited or relied on by a court or a party in any other action — so the requested persuasive-authority footnote would be improper.

#### Proposition `abstention-006-p3`

> Under California Rules of Court, rule 8.1115(a), except as provided in (b), an unpublished opinion of a California Court of Appeal or superior court appellate division must not be cited or relied on by a court or a party in any other action.

**Verbatim authoritative excerpt relied on:**
