### C1. `research-019-c1`

**Criterion:** States the Evidence Code section 1152(a) rule that evidence a person has, in compromise or from humanitarian motives, furnished, offered, or promised to furnish money or another thing, act, or service, as well as any conduct or statements made in negotiation thereof, is inadmissible to prove liability for the loss or damage.

#### Proposition `research-019-p1`

> Evidence that a person has, in compromise or from humanitarian motives, furnished or offered or promised to furnish money or any other thing, act, or service to another who has sustained or claims loss or damage, as well as any conduct or statements made in negotiation thereof, is inadmissible to prove liability for the loss or damage or any part of it.

**Verbatim authoritative excerpt relied on:**

```text
Evidence that a person has, in compromise or from humanitarian motives, furnished or offered or promised to furnish money or any other thing, act, or service to another who has sustained or will sustain or claims that he or she has sustained or will sustain loss or damage, as well as any conduct or statements made in negotiation thereof, is inadmissible to prove his or her liability for the loss or damage or any part of it.
```

### C2. `research-019-c2`

**Criterion:** Notes the section 1152(b) limitation that, outside the specified bad-faith and Insurance Code section 790.03(h) actions, evidence of settlement offers is not admitted in a motion for a new trial, in any additur or remittitur proceeding, or on appeal.

#### Proposition `research-019-p2`

> Other than as may be admitted in an action for breach of the covenant of good faith and fair dealing or violation of subdivision (h) of Section 790.03 of the Insurance Code, evidence of settlement offers shall not be admitted in a motion for a new trial, in any proceeding involving an additur or remittitur, or on appeal.

**Verbatim authoritative excerpt relied on:**

### C4. `research-019-c4`

**Criterion:** States the separate Evidence Code section 1119(a) bar: no evidence of anything said or any admission made for the purpose of, in the course of, or pursuant to, a mediation or a mediation consultation is admissible or subject to discovery, and disclosure shall not be compelled.

#### Proposition `research-019-p4`

> No evidence of anything said or any admission made for the purpose of, in the course of, or pursuant to, a mediation or a mediation consultation is admissible or subject to discovery, and disclosure of the evidence shall not be compelled, in any noncriminal proceeding in which testimony can be compelled to be given.
