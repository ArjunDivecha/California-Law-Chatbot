Brand exploration · 21 Aug 2026

Twelve marks for DancingElephant

Replacements for the current thin-line elephant. Every concept is tested at 28 px (sidebar) and 16 px (favicon) — the sizes where the current logo dissolves — and shown once on the dark-plum lockup. Concepts 1–2 keep the animal, 3 nods to it; 4–6 keep only the idea (the line that never breaks, memory, verification). Round two (07–12) pushes wider: literal marks (a footprint, a chat-bubble elephant, a faceted head) and lawyer-insider abstractions (a pilcrow, a seal, an ear-as-fingerprint). All vector, all drawn from the committed palette: violet #7C5CFC, teal #2DD4BF, amber #E8A05C, plum-ink #2A2233.
