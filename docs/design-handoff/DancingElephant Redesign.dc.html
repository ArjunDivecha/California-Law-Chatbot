My client Maria Delgado was served with a complaint on June 3. Can we still bring an anti-SLAPP motion, and does the 60-day clock run from service or filing?

Yes — you're within the window, but barely. Under CCP § 425.16(f), an anti-SLAPP motion must be filed within 60 days of service of the complaint, not filing 1. Service on June 3 puts your deadline at August 2.

Courts may permit a later filing at their discretion, but Platypus Wear, Inc. v. Goldberg holds that discretion is abused where the delay is substantial and unjustified 2.

04 · Consent / attestation modal
Before you continue

A one-minute read on how DancingElephant handles client information.

What stays on this device. When you type client facts, names and identifiers are replaced with tokens in your browser. The map from tokens back to real names never leaves this device.
What is sent. Only the tokenized text reaches the model and research providers — never the real names. Model inputs are not used for training.
Your obligations stand. This is a technical safeguard, not a substitute for your duties under Rule of Professional Conduct 1.6 or your duty to supervise AI-assisted work.
No recovery. Clearing browser data deletes the local token map permanently. Prior chats will show tokens in place of names. This is deliberate.

Plaintiff's motion should be denied for three independent reasons. First, the motion is untimely under Code of Civil Procedure section 2031.310, subdivision (c), which requires notice within 45 days of the verified response.

Second, the separate statement fails to comply with rule 3.1345 of the California Rules of Court because it omits the text of each response at issue, an independent ground for denial.

Third, Defendant has already produced all responsive, non-privileged documents in its possession, custody, or control, and Plaintiff identifies no specific deficiency.

For these reasons, and as set forth in the accompanying declaration, the Court should deny the motion and award Defendant its reasonable expenses incurred in opposing it.
